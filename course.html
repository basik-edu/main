Tangible Transformation

From Stage Hesitation to Unstoppable Presence

Public speaking is not an innate gift—it is a repeatable skill. Here is how your child's confidence evolves in 30 days.

Day 1 • Before Basik 🌧️

Nervous & Hesitant

  • ✕ Freezes up when called upon in classroom discussions or school assembly.
  • ✕ Frequent filler words ("um, uh, like") and rushing through sentences.
  • ✕ Looks at feet, fiddles with hands, and struggles with eye contact.
  • ✕ Memorizes speech scripts word-for-word and panics when losing place.

Poised, Fluent & Inspiring

  • ✓ Confidently volunteers for podium speeches, school debates, and presentations.
  • ✓ Deliberate vocal pauses, rhythmic pacing, and crisp pronunciation.
  • ✓ Commanding posture, open gestures, and warm audience eye engagement.
  • ✓ Thinks on their feet using structured storytelling frameworks.
The Basik Method

The Four Core Pillars of Basik Speaker

A holistic architecture engineered specifically for young minds to replace fear with authentic self-expression.

01 / PILLAR

One-to-One Mentorship

No generic mass lectures. Every student is paired with a dedicated speech coach who diagnoses vocal hesitation, refines tonality, and provides individual video feedback.

  • Personalized pace matching your child's personality
  • Continuous constructive video speech reviews
02 / PILLAR

Daily Speaking Missions

Confidence comes through repetition. Students undertake exciting 60-minute daily speaking challenges—recording mini-speeches, spontaneous responses, and vocal drills.

  • Gamified prompts: "Pitch an Invention", "Defend an Opinion"
  • Converts public speaking into a natural daily reflex
03 / PILLAR

Live Masterclasses & Tasks

Interactive masterclasses break down speech architecture: irresistible hooks, memorable 3-point body structures, body language cues, and how to command audience silence.

  • Learn proven storytelling formulas (Hero's Journey, STAR)
  • Live real-time teacher corrections & roleplay drills
04 / PILLAR

Weekly Speaking Gatherings

A supportive, warm stage every single week where students present live in front of mentors and peers. They experience real applause, constructive feedback, and stage presence.

  • Safe, high-energy environment with zero judgment
  • Gradual exposure that systematically eliminates stage fright
Curriculum Journey

Your Child's 30-Day Step-by-Step Roadmap

Explore the four progressive weeks designed to build lasting public speaking instincts.

Days 1 to 7

Conquering Butterflies & Finding Your Voice

We start by dismantling the fear of judgment. Students learn deep diaphragmatic breathing, posture grounding, and craft their very first 60-second self-introduction speech.

🎯 Milestone: 60-Second Video Self-Intro Reviewed by Mentor
Day 1–2
Ice-Breaking & Self-Discovery Understanding your unique speaking style & baseline assessment.
Day 3–4
Breath Support & Stage Posture Eliminating shaky voices through anchor breathing techniques.
Day 5–7
The First Mini-Speech Delivering your introductory story to your 1-on-1 mentor.
Days 8 to 14

Structuring Speeches That Captivate

Great speakers don't ramble—they organize. Students master powerful narrative frameworks: catchy opening hooks, the "Rule of Three" body structure, and punchy calls-to-action.

🎯 Milestone: 2-Minute Persuasive Pitch Delivery
Day 8–10
Crafting Irresistible Openers How to grab the audience within the first 10 seconds.
Day 11–12
The Power of Storytelling (STAR Model) Turning personal anecdotes into inspiring life lessons.
Day 13–14
Memorable Closings Ending with clarity that leaves people thinking for hours.
Days 15 to 21

Vocal Artistry & Non-Verbal Power

Over 70% of communication is non-verbal. In Week 3, students train their vocal dynamics—inflection, pacing, dramatic pauses, and purposeful hand gestures that magnify their points.

🎯 Milestone: Impromptu Speech without Filler Words
Day 15–17
Eliminating Fillers ("Um", "Ah") Replacing nervous hesitations with confident silences.
Day 18–19
Body Language & Stage Movement Eye contact scanning patterns, open hands, and commanding space.
Day 20–21
Impromptu "Think-on-Feet" Drills Speaking spontaneously on surprise topics with zero prep time.
Days 22 to 30

Capstone Showcase & Graduation

The grand culmination! Students prepare a full 3-minute capstone keynote speech, rehearse with their mentor, and deliver it live in front of the community gathering.

🎯 Milestone: Live Capstone Speech & Basik Certification
Day 22–25
Drafting the Capstone Speech Fine-tuning every line with 1-on-1 mentor guidance.
Day 26–28
Dress Rehearsals & Timing Polishing pitch, delivery tempo, and audio/video setups.
Day 29–30
The Grand Graduation Showcase Delivering on the live stage, receiving certificate & honors.
